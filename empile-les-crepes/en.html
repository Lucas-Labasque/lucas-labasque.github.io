One tap, endless pancakes

A pancake slides above the plate: tap to drop it on the stack. Anything that hangs over is sliced off and the stack gets narrower. Rounds last from 30 seconds to 2 minutes and can be played with one hand, perfect for a quick break.

See the game

How to play

  • Tap anywhere to drop the pancake
  • The part that hangs over is sliced off: the more precise you are, the wider the pancake stays
  • If the pancake misses the stack completely, it's game over
  • Your score is the number of pancakes stacked, and your high score is saved

What's inside

  • "Perfect!": within a few pixels, the pancake snaps into place with no cut
  • 3 perfects in a row and the pancake gets wider
  • A new topping every 10 pancakes: chocolate, strawberry, caramel, pistachio…
  • Scenery that goes from morning to sunset, then a starry night
  • Increasing speed, tasty sounds and haptics

FAQ

What is a "Perfect"?

If your pancake lands within 6 pixels of the one below, it snaps into place and keeps its full width. Three perfects in a row make it a little wider.

My progress is gone

Your high score and the sound setting are stored only on your phone. They are erased if you delete the game and don't move between phones.

The game has no sound

Check the sound button in the menu, then make sure your phone isn't in silent mode.

Ads and privacy

There are never ads during a round: only an occasional ad between rounds, and an optional video to keep playing after a loss. In Europe, your consent is requested on first launch; on iPhone, ad tracking can also be managed in Settings > Privacy & Security > Tracking. See the privacy policy.
