FAQ

How do combos work?

Tap pigeons less than a second apart: the 3rd in a row gives x2, the 6th x3, up to x5. Wait too long or let a pigeon escape and the combo resets.

How do I unlock looks and settings?

Every round adds your score to your points, and completed challenges give bonus points. Spend them with the palette button in the menu.

My progress is gone

High scores, points and unlocks are stored only on your phone. They are erased if you delete the game and don't move between phones.

Ads and privacy

There are never ads during a round: only an occasional ad between rounds, and an optional video to keep playing after a loss. In Europe, your consent is requested on first launch; on iPhone, ad tracking can also be managed in Settings > Privacy & Security > Tracking. See the privacy policy.
