Chores, room by room

Plan the chores of each room, share them with your household and get a reminder at the right time. Chore done? Mark it with one tap and the next due date is worked out for you. The app itself is in French.

A look at the app

What you get

  • Chores by room, with their frequency and next due date
  • Three views: rooms, list and calendar
  • A shared household: invite people with a code and assign chores
  • Reminders at the time you choose
  • A vacation mode that pauses reminders and postpones chores
  • No ads, and usable without an account on your device

Frequently asked questions

Do I need an account?

No. Choose "Utiliser sans compte, sur cet appareil" and your chores stay on your phone. An Apple or Google account is needed to share a household and find your data on another device.

How do I join a household?

Sign in, open the Foyers tab, tap "Rejoindre avec un code" and enter the invitation code you received.

I don't get reminders

Check that notifications are on in the app's Réglages tab, then in your phone settings. Vacation mode also pauses reminders.

I can't sign in

Check your internet connection and try again. If it keeps happening, email us and tell us which service you used (Apple or Google).

How do I delete my account?

In the Profil tab, tap "Supprimer mon compte". You can also request it by email. See the privacy policy for details.
