FAQ

How do I earn coins?

Each launch gives you the coins you grabbed in the air, plus one coin every 10 meters. Golden eggs are worth 10 coins, and completed challenges give bonus coins.

What are boosts for?

In the air, each tap uses one boost: a wing flap at first, then a jetpack thrust once you have bought it. The "Boosts" upgrade gives you more of them.

My progress is gone

Your best distance, coins, upgrades and looks are stored only on your phone. They are erased if you delete the game and do not move from one phone to another.

There is no sound

Check the sound button at the bottom of the menu, then make sure your phone is not in silent mode.

Ads and privacy

There are never ads during a flight: only an occasional ad between two launches, and optional videos to double your coins or get extra boosts. In Europe, your consent is asked at first launch; on iPhone, ad tracking can also be managed in Settings > Privacy & Security > Tracking. Details in the privacy policy.
