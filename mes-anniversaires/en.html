All your loved ones' birthdays in one place

Import birthdays from your contacts or add them by hand, then let the app remind you at the right time. On the day, a celebration screen is waiting and your birthday message is already written.

Everything stays on your phone: no account, no ads, no tracking.

The app at a glance

Everything to send your wishes on time

  • Widgets: the next birthdays or the month calendar on your home screen.
  • Lock screen: a countdown to the next birthday.
  • Reminders: a notification on the day, at the time you choose.
  • Wishes in one tap: a customizable message with the first name and age, ready to send by text.
  • Contacts: import and sync birthdays from your address book.
  • Calendar: the month at a glance and an Upcoming list with days remaining.
  • Cards: photo, favourites, notes and gift ideas; age and star sign are worked out for you.

How it works

  1. Add your loved ones. Import them from your contacts or enter their birthday.
  2. Get a reminder. The app lets you know on the day and the widgets show what's coming.
  3. Send your wishes. Your message is ready with the first name and age: just send it.

Frequently asked questions

Is the app free?

Yes, with no ads and no account to create.

Where is my data stored?

Only on your phone. Names, dates, notes and photos are never sent to a server.

Do I have to give access to my contacts?

No, it's optional. Access is only used to import birthdays; you can also enter everything by hand.

Is the app available in English?

Not yet: the interface is in French. Dates and names work the same in any language.
